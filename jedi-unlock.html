Path to the Force

Jedi Unlock Guide

The classic Holocron journey — master professions, meditate at a shrine, and unlock your Force-sensitive destiny.

🔮 Step 1: Hunt the 4 Holocrons

📜 How Holocrons Work

  • ✓ Holocrons are rare drops from high-level mobs
  • ✓ Each holocron reveals ONE random profession
  • ✓ You must master 4 professions total
  • ✓ After mastering 4 professions, you are ready to meditate

⚔️ All Professions Count

  • ✓ All professions now count toward unlock
  • ✓ Combat, crafting, social — any profession works
  • ✓ No restrictions on which professions you choose
  • ✓ Master any 4 professions of your choice

✅ All professions accepted — choose your path!

🧘 Step 2: Meditate at a Shrine

🌟 What to Do

  • 1. After mastering 4 professions, travel to a Force Shrine (Dathomir, Yavin IV, etc.)
  • 2. Use the /meditate command at the shrine
  • 3. Wait for the meditation to complete
  • 4. You will receive a new character slot

⚡ What You Get

  • ✓ +1 Character Slot — reserved for your Jedi
  • ✓ Your new character is Force Sensitive
  • ✓ You can now create your Jedi character
  • ✓ No profession unlocks needed — you are ready to begin

⚔️ Step 3: Grind Your Jedi Skills

📘 Post-Publish 6 Jedi Skills

  • • Force Sensitivity
  • • Force Abilities (Push, Pull, Lightning, Heal)
  • • Lightsaber Combat (all forms)
  • • Jedi Defense and Mitigation
  • • Force Run and Jump

🔧 How to Grind

  • 1. Use your Jedi skills to gain XP
  • 2. Each skill has a progression tree
  • 3. Master all force skills to unlock the Knight Trials
  • 4. No Padawan Trials needed — you are already a Padawan

🏛️ Step 4: The Knight Trials

After mastering your Jedi skills, take on the Knight Trials to earn the title of Jedi Knight.

Tusken Raiders

Kill 19 Tusken Raiders

📍 Tatooine — Fort Tusken

Ancient Bull Rancor

Kill 8 Ancient Bull Rancors

📍 Dathomir — Mutant Rancor Cave

Stintaril Prowler

Kill 3 Stintaril Prowlers

📍 Yavin IV — Giant Stintaril lairs

Blurrg Raptor

Kill 3 Blurrg Raptors

📍 Endor — Blurrg Hunter missions

Enraged Kimogila

Kill 2 Enraged Kimogilas

📍 Lok — Kimogila missions (group CL 95+)

Peko Peko Albatross

Kill 2 Peko Peko Albatrosses

📍 Naboo — Giant Peko Peko missions

Graul Marauder

Kill 2 Graul Marauders

📍 Dantooine — Frenzied Graul missions

⚖️ Light or Dark — Council Trial

Make your choice at the Council

Choose: Light Side or Dark Side

Enemy Soldier

Kill 15 enemy commandos

Rebel: Storm Commandos | Imperial: Rebel Commandos

🏆 Upon completing all 9 trials, you will be granted the title of Jedi Knight or Dark Jedi Knight and become eligible for the Force Ranking System (FRS).
